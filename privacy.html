Privacy Policy

TCS UploadFit is designed to prepare images on your device. The app does not upload your selected images to TCS servers.

Image files

When you select images, your browser or installed app reads and processes those files locally. The image pixels are rendered on your device and exported files are created locally. TCS does not receive, store, or inspect the contents of your images.

Metadata

Exported images are re-encoded from their visible pixels. Original EXIF and other source metadata are not intentionally copied to exported files. This can remove camera details, GPS metadata, and some color/profile information.

Local settings

Your language choice and saved presets may be stored in local browser storage on your device. They are not synchronized to TCS.

Network requests

The app shell and updates are delivered from the website or Microsoft Store package host. The image-processing workflow does not require an image upload endpoint, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or third-party image-processing service.

Children

TCS UploadFit is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app. For privacy questions, contact thechocostudio@gmail.com.
